10. Decompressed it using "bunzip2":

11. Checked the file again:

12. Repeated the decompression steps (renaming when necessary) until I reached a tar archive.  
13. Extracted the tar file:  
    tar -xf binary
     
14. Since the file had been recursively compressed using gzip, bzip2, and tar, I kept repeating the process:  
    1. "ls" to check files  
    2. "file filename" to identify the compression type  
    3. Used the appropriate decompression command ("gunzip", "bunzip2", "tar -xf")  

15. Eventually, I got to the final password file, named "data8":

MY STRUGGLES AND WHAT I LEARNT
